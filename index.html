The solution

Scoring you can audit line by line

Every metric is percentile-ranked across the universe, then combined into five published factor scores:

  • Quality = avg pct( ROCE ↑, health score ↑, gross margin ↑, net debt/equity ↓, interest cover ↑ )
  • Value = avg pct( P/E ↓, P/BV ↓, free cash flow yield ↑, dividend yield ↑ )
  • Momentum = avg pct( 12m price change ↑, 6m price change ↑, RSI(14) ↑ )
  • Growth = avg pct( EPS growth ↑, revenue growth ↑, forward EPS growth ↑ )
  • Small Cap = pct( 1 / market cap )

Live definitions: ROCE = EBIT ÷ average (shareholders' equity + total debt) over the latest two annual reports; interest cover = EBIT ÷ interest expense; health score counts how many of nine published thresholds a company clears. London listings are converted from pence to pounds before any ratio is taken. A metric a company doesn't report is scored a neutral 50 — it is never estimated.

Your headline FactorRank is a weighted blend you control — drag the sliders in the screener and the entire table re-ranks instantly. The default is the academically-backed 35% Quality / 25% Value / 25% Momentum / 10% Growth / 5% Size blend.
